# ORB + VWAP Filter — 0DTE Backtest (2025 & 2026)

Historical replay of the live bot (`orb_vwap_filter.py`) on **real QQQ 0DTE option
1-minute prices**, reproduced by [`backtest_vwap_filter.py`](backtest_vwap_filter.py).

The backtest is faithful to the live rules: 09:30–09:44 opening range, session VWAP
filter, breakout entry, strike = `round(entry) ± 3` ($1 spacing), 3 contracts scaled
out 1-at-a-time at **+50% / +100% / +150%** of premium, **−50%** hybrid stop on the
remainder, opposite-breakout exit, 13:00 ET force-flat, $1,000 daily-loss cap, and a
15-minute re-entry cooldown after a stop-out.

P&L is **per the 3-contract position, in dollars, with NO commissions or slippage.**

The strategy hits its −50% stop on **~2 out of 3 trades**. The ~18–20% of trades that
run to the full +150% target carry the book, but in both years they did not fully
cover the steady stream of stops.

## Methodology & Caveats

- **Data.** Underlying = QQQ 1-min RTH bars (repo `qqq_1m_2018_2026.csv.xz`, supplemented
  by monthly backups for Apr–Jun 2026). Options = real 0DTE QQQ 1-min OHLC
  (Thetadata parquet). Entry premium = option close at the signal minute; intrabar
  exits fill at the target/stop price (option high/low crossing), opposite/EOD exits at
  the bar close.
- **No transaction costs.** Commissions and slippage are **not** modelled. At ~$0.65/
  contract/side, a typical round trip (3 in, up to 3 out) is ≈$4–6; across 494 trades
  that alone is roughly **−$2,000+** of additional drag in 2025. Buying at the ask
  (vs. the close used here) makes entries worse still. **Real P&L is materially below
  the figures above.**
- **Conservative intrabar ordering.** Within a bar the −50% stop is checked before profit
  targets, so ambiguous up-and-down bars resolve pessimistically.
- **Re-entry.** After any exit, a new entry requires the underlying to trade back inside
  the opening range first; after a stop-out the 15-minute cooldown also applies.
- **2026 is partial** (through 2026-06-12) and excludes ~12 late-March sessions whose
  underlying source lacked volume (VWAP could not be computed).

## Reproduce

```bash
cd ibkr/orb_vwap_filter
python3 backtest_vwap_filter.py 2025 2026   # prints tables, writes backtest_results_2025_2026.json
```

## Bottom line

On real 0DTE option prices, the ORB + VWAP filter strategy was **roughly breakeven in
2025 and losing in 2026 before costs** — and **net-negative in both years once realistic
commissions and slippage are included.** The edge measured in the underlying-only ORB
research (see the repo's main `README.md`) does not survive translation into long 0DTE
premium with a −50% stop and capped scale-out targets. Before risking capital, the exit
structure (stop distance, target ladder, strike offset) needs optimization, and the test
should be re-run with commissions and ask-side fills.
